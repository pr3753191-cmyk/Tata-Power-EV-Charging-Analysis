# Tata Power EV Charging Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes Tata Power EV charging data using **Microsoft Excel** to understand EV charging demand, station utilization, customer behavior, energy consumption, revenue performance, and future infrastructure requirements.

The analysis is presented through interactive Excel dashboards and visualizations to identify important business trends and operational insights.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze EV charging operations and answer key business questions such as:

* How is EV charging demand changing over time?
* Which locations have higher charging activity?
* Are charging stations and plugs being utilized efficiently?
* What are the charging patterns throughout the day?
* How do different customer income groups contribute to charging usage?
* How do EV models and battery capacities affect energy consumption?
* Which areas may require additional charging infrastructure?
* What factors can support future expansion and investment decisions?

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Business & Usage Overview

This section focuses on overall charging demand, energy consumption, revenue generation, active customers, and charging behavior over time.

Key metrics from the analysis include:

* **114,000** total charging sessions
* **2.85+ million kWh** energy consumed
* **49.69 million** total revenue
* **8,000** active customers
* **₹435.91** average revenue per session
* **25.09 kWh** average energy consumption per session

The analysis also examines charging patterns by day and time to understand demand behavior.

### 2. Station Utilization & Operational Efficiency

This section analyzes charging station performance and infrastructure utilization.

The analysis covers:

* Sessions per station
* Average utilization per station
* Plug availability versus usage
* Average kWh per station
* District-wise station density
* Sessions per plug

High-demand locations can be identified for potential capacity expansion, while underutilized stations can be evaluated for operational improvement.

### 3. Customer & Demand Behavior

This section analyzes customer charging behavior and revenue contribution.

The analysis includes:

* Unique customers
* Sessions per customer
* Average kWh per customer
* Revenue per customer
* Income-tier based charging behavior
* EV model analysis
* Battery capacity and energy consumption

The analysis shows that charging usage is distributed across different income groups, with the **Mid-Range** customer segment contributing approximately **36%** of overall charging usage.

### 4. Financial Performance & Expansion Strategy

The project also considers financial and expansion-related metrics such as:

* Project CAPEX
* Annual Revenue
* EBITDA
* Payback Period
* 5-Year ROI
* Existing Stations vs Projected EVs
* Demand-Supply Gap

These metrics can support data-driven decisions regarding future EV charging infrastructure expansion.

## 🔍 Key Insights

* EV charging demand remains consistently active across the analyzed period.
* Charging activity is comparatively higher during weekdays.
* Charging demand is concentrated in several high-usage districts.
* Daytime hours, particularly around **8 AM–4 PM**, show stronger charging activity.
* Some high-demand stations may require additional charging capacity.
* Underutilized stations may require operational optimization.
* The customer base shows strong repeat charging behavior.
* Mid-Range income customers contribute the largest share of charging usage.
* Higher battery-capacity EV models generally show higher energy consumption.
* District-level demand analysis can help identify locations for future infrastructure expansion.

## 🚀 Future Scope

Potential future improvements include:

* Expanding charging infrastructure in high-demand locations
* Prioritizing fast and ultra-fast chargers in high-usage areas
* Improving utilization of underperforming stations
* Using demand-supply analysis for new charging hubs
* Exploring solar energy and battery storage integration
* Applying predictive analytics for maintenance and demand forecasting
* Using ROI, EV growth, revenue, and utilization data for expansion planning
